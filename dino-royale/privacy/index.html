Dino Royale Privacy Policy

Last Updated: September 27, 2026

Who We Are

Dino Royale is an independent game currently in development, created by Dinosaur Inc. This policy explains how information is handled in connection with Dino Royale's online multiplayer features.

Epic Online Services & Epic Account Services

Dino Royale uses Epic Online Services and Epic Account Services to provide online multiplayer and account-related functionality. When a player chooses to sign in with an Epic Account, Epic may provide Dino Royale with information authorized through the permissions the player grants. Dino Royale currently requests the following permissions:

  • Basic Profile — basic Epic Account profile information
  • Friends List — the player's Epic Account friends list
  • Presence — online/status information for social features

How This Information Is Used

Information obtained through Epic Account Services is used only as needed to support authentication, multiplayer sessions, friend and social functionality, lobby functionality, and related in-game features. It is not used for any purpose beyond operating these features of Dino Royale.

What We Do Not Do

  • Dino Royale does not currently sell personal information.
  • Dino Royale does not currently use this information for targeted advertising.
  • Dino Royale does not currently process payments through this website.

Epic Games' Own Policies

Epic Games separately collects and processes information in connection with Epic Account Services according to Epic's own privacy policy and terms of service. This policy covers only how Dino Royale itself uses information obtained through those services and does not describe Epic's own data practices.

Changes to This Policy

Dino Royale is still in development, and this privacy policy may be updated as the game and its features develop. Any changes will be posted on this page.
